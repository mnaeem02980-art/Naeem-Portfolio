⚠️ Chrome Mein Microphone Block Hua Hai (Permission Blocked)

Google Chrome ne is page ke liye microphone band kiya hua hai. Isko theek karne ke do aasan tariqe hain:

Tariqa 1 (Chrome Settings):
1. Browser ke upar URL bar mai 🎚️ Tune/Settings ya 🔒 Lock icon par click karein.
2. Microphone ko "Allow" par set karein.
3. Page ko Reload (F5) karein.
Tariqa 2: Live HTTPS Par Kholein (Is Par Mic Kaam Karega)
Upar button par click karein aur bolna shuru karein — text foran likhta jayega!
Shortcut: Space ya Ctrl + Space se start/stop karein
